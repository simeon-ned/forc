# Lecture 01 figure provenance

These eight PNGs are unchanged copies of figures already included in this repository under `_legacy/content/_sort/01_modeling/images/`:

- `forc_robot_scheme.png`: `schemes/forc_robot_scheme.png`.
- `robot_to_model_blank.png`, `mbk_eq.png`, `pend_eq.png`, `lander_eq.png`, `srb_eq.png`, `satt_eq.png`, `cart_pole_eq.png`: the corresponding files in `systems/`.

They are reused in the same modeling lecture, with descriptive alternatives and captions translating the archived notation to the current text. Embedded photographs and other third-party elements retain their original rights; this provenance record does not assert new authorship or a separate license for them.

The equations written in the lecture text define its conventions. In particular, distinguish mass flow from thrust in the lander, a gravity bias from physical gravitational acceleration in the floating-body figure, tangential force from angular generalized torque in the satellite, and cart actuation from pole actuation. The archived image files themselves have not been edited.
